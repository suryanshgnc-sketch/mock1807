    VIIFAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is MDCCCVII free?

    Yes. It is currently free and carries no advertising.

    When do tests start?

    Each test has a scheduled start and unlocks automatically for all students at once.

    How is my score calculated?

    On our servers, after the answer key is published. Keys can be corrected, which may re-evaluate scores and ranks.

    Who can see my data?

    Other students see your name, photo and scores on the leaderboard. Your email is never shown. See the Privacy Policy.

    Is this affiliated with NTA?

    No. MDCCCVII is an independent practice platform, not affiliated with or endorsed by NTA or any coaching institute.
