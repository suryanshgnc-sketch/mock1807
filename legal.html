01 · ABOUT

About MDCCCVII

MDCCCVII Tests is an independent online computer-based test platform for JEE aspirants. Students attempt scheduled, timed mock tests in an exam-style interface, receive results once the answer key is published, and see their standing on a live leaderboard.

The platform is designed, developed and operated by suryansh1807 ("MDCCCVII", "we", "us"). The service is currently free to use and carries no advertising.

03 · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data we handle and why. We aim to follow the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act): collect only what is needed, use it only for stated purposes, keep it secure, and let you exercise your rights.

Data we collect

DataSourcePurpose
Name, email address, profile photoGoogle sign-inAuthentication, identifying you on scorecards and the leaderboard
Profile details you enter (e.g. display name, roll number)YouCandidate display in the test interface
Test attempts: responses, timings, marks, submission timeYour use of testsEvaluation, scorecards, analysis, rankings, integrity checks
Basic technical data (browser, device type, request logs, IP address)Automatically, via hosting and backend providersSecurity, abuse prevention, debugging

How we use it

To authenticate you, deliver and evaluate tests, compute results and rankings, detect cheating or abuse, keep the service secure, and respond to your requests. We do not sell your personal data, do not show advertising, and do not use your data for profiling by third parties.

Leaderboard visibility

Your name, photo and scores are visible to other signed-in students on the leaderboard. Your email address is never shown.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase – database, authentication and server-side evaluation.
  • Google – sign-in (Google Identity) and Google Fonts for typography.
  • jsDelivr (CDN) – delivers the open-source Supabase client library to your browser.
  • Static hosting provider – serves the website files.

These providers may process data on servers outside India. They act under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not share your data with anyone else except where required by law or to protect our rights and users.

Retention

Account and attempt data is kept while your account is active so you retain access to your result history. On a deletion request (see below) we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we must retain limited records for security, dispute resolution or legal compliance. Backups may take a short additional time to expire.

Security

Answer keys are stored server-side and are not sent to your browser during a test. Data is transmitted over HTTPS and access is controlled through database security rules. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting your personal data occurs we will notify affected users and the relevant authority as required by law.

Your rights

You may request to access a summary of your data, correct inaccurate data, erase your data and account, withdraw consent for processing (which may prevent use of the service), and make a grievance. Email suryanshsharma1807@gmail.com from your registered address. You may also nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf as provided under the DPDP Act. If you are unsatisfied with our response you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

Consent. By signing in you consent to the processing described here. You can withdraw consent at any time by requesting deletion.

05 · STUDENTS UNDER 18

Students under 18

Many JEE aspirants are minors. Under the DPDP Act, processing the personal data of a person under 18 requires verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian. If you are under 18, please use MDCCCVII only with your parent or guardian's knowledge and consent. We do not track or profile minors, do not show them advertising, and do not use their data for anything other than running the tests.

A parent or guardian may email us at any time to review or delete a child's data. If we learn that a minor's data was collected without required consent, we will delete it.

06 · FAIR PLAY

Fair Play Policy

  • Attempt every test yourself, alone, within the scheduled window and time limit.
  • No external help, answer sharing, screenshots or recordings of test content, automation, or multiple accounts.
  • Do not attempt to access answer keys or other students' responses.

We may review attempts (timing patterns, duplicate accounts, unusual similarity). Violations may lead to removal from rankings, score cancellation, or account suspension. Decisions on integrity are made in good faith by the platform owner and are final, but you may write to us to contest one.
