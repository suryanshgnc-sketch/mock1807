MULTIPLE-CORRECT KEY FORMAT:
  Example:
    A,C,1+3,B,4.25,2+4,...
  A+C, A|C, A&C and A/C are accepted by the admin UI and normalized to 1+3.
  Numerical questions stay numeric.

  The admin panel uploads it automatically. Students can access it after they have submitted that test.
